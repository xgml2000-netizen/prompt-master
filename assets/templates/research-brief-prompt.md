# Research Brief Prompt Template

```markdown
Act as a research analyst. Create a concise, source-grounded brief about [topic].

Research goal:
[What decision or understanding this research should support]

Scope:
- Geography: [region]
- Time range: [current, last 12 months, historical, etc.]
- Include: [required angles]
- Exclude: [out-of-scope areas]

Requirements:
- Prioritize primary or authoritative sources.
- Distinguish facts from interpretation.
- Include dates for time-sensitive claims.
- Note uncertainty and conflicting evidence.
- Do not overstate conclusions.

Output format:
## Executive Summary
[3-5 bullets]

## Key Findings
| Finding | Evidence | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Risks and Unknowns
- [Risk or unknown]

## Recommended Next Steps
1. [Action]
```

## 中文版

```markdown
请你作为研究分析师，围绕[主题]输出一份简洁、有来源支撑的研究简报。

研究目标：
[这份研究要支持什么判断或决策]

范围：
- 地区：[地区]
- 时间范围：[当前、过去 12 个月、历史等]
- 包含：[必须覆盖的角度]
- 排除：[不在范围内的内容]

要求：
- 优先使用一手或权威来源。
- 区分事实和解读。
- 对时效性信息标注具体日期。
- 标注不确定性和冲突证据。
- 不要夸大结论。

输出格式：
## 摘要
[3-5 条要点]

## 关键发现
| 发现 | 证据 | 来源 |
| --- | --- | --- |

## 风险与未知
- [风险或未知]

## 建议下一步
1. [行动]
```
